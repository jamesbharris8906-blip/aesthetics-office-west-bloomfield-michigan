Lasers & Skin Rejuvenation

Laser care in this office covers spots, redness, texture, and hair. The first step is always a close look at your skin and a talk about sun and past burns. Injectables include Botox, Dysport, and fillers, and PRF uses a small amount of your own blood to help skin heal and look fuller. The talk before any needle is key. You will talk about where you want a soft change, where you want to leave things alone, and how long a result may last. Lip Fillers

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect when I walk in?

You will be greeted, asked about your skin, and given time to talk before any treatment. The team will look at your skin in good light, ask about sun and past care, and lay out a few clear choices. You can ask questions, take the plan home, or book a treatment that day.

Can I get Botox and a facial on the same day?

Sometimes yes, and sometimes the team will space them out. It depends on your skin, the type of facial, and what you want to fix first. Tell the team what you have in mind, and they will plan a day that is safe and simple for your skin.

How do I know if XERF fits me?

A talk is the best way to tell. The team will look at how loose the skin is, talk about your goals, and say if XERF, a laser, or another choice fits better. XERF is a good topic if you want firming without needles or downtime.

Do fillers look natural?

They can, when the team uses small amounts and looks at your whole face. The aim here is a fresh, rested look, not a changed face. Tell the team if you want a very light change, and they will start small and check in with you later.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Tell the team right away. Sensitive skin needs a gentler plan, and some peels, lasers, or products may not fit you. The team will pick a mild first step, test how your skin reacts, and build from there only if your skin stays calm.

How often should I get a facial?

Many people get a facial every four to six weeks, but it depends on your skin and your budget. Some people come in before a big event, while others keep a steady plan all year. The team will help you pick a pace that fits your skin and your week.
